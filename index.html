About Me

A curious mind with a love for creativity and technology.

Hi there! I'm Angelica Garcia, a third-year Information Technology student at City College of Calamba, exploring the space where creativity meets technology.

I've always been curious about how ideas can turn into something visually meaningful. Whether it's designing logos, creating posters, crafting social media graphics, or experimenting with digital layouts, I enjoy the process of bringing ideas to life.
